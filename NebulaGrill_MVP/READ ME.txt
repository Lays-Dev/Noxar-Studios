NEBULA GRILL READ ME

Download the zip file to your phone. Go to your download location and extract the file. Run the .apk file. If a warning pops up, continue download. If your download location is blocking unknown files, go to your settings to change it.


Tap the patron to take an order. Tap the buttons on the left to change scenes. Tap the fridge to make the patrons order. Tap the patron a second time to give them their order.


Current features include taking orders, building orders, fulfilling orders, and receiving payment.

No outside assets used.
